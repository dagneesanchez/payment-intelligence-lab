# Payment Intelligence Lab

A practical research lab focused on payments, fintech, monetization and digital product strategy.

## About the project

The Payment Intelligence Lab is a personal initiative created to study how payment products, business models and financial ecosystems work across Latin America, Europe and global markets.

The goal is to transform research into practical product insights, frameworks, case studies and data-driven analyses.

## Research areas

- Payment ecosystems
- Issuing and acquiring
- Digital wallets
- Payment orchestration
- Cross-border payments
- Fraud and risk
- Monetization models
- Product strategy
- Payment metrics and KPIs
- Fintech trends in Latin America and Europe
